### Abstract

The Feigenbaum constants $\delta = 4.669201609102990671853\ldots$ and $\alpha = 2.502907875095892822283\ldots$ govern the universal geometry of period-doubling cascades in unimodal families. Their existence and universality are theorems - computer-assisted since Lanford (1982), conceptual since Lyubich (1999) - yet absolutely nothing is known about their arithmetic nature: not a closed form, not transcendence, not even irrationality. The problem is matched to current AI methods because its tractable faces are certified computation and symbolic mining: rigorous functional-equation enclosures in ball arithmetic, record certified digits, pre-registered integer-relation sweeps with machine-checkable exclusion certificates, and structural manipulation of the Cvitanović–Feigenbaum equation. The complete resolution defined in section 2 is the target; it is candidly beyond current mathematics, and the graded targets of section 3 - including proofs that $\delta$ lies in *no* specific closed-form class up to explicit complexity bounds - are the intended product. Anything less than section 2 must be reported as a partial result, never as a solution.

## 1. Exact problem statement

### 1.1 The fixed point and the constants

Consider even, real-analytic maps $g$ on a neighborhood of $[-1,1]$, unimodal with a nondegenerate quadratic critical point at $0$, normalized by $g(0) = 1$. The **Cvitanović–Feigenbaum equation** is

\[
g(x) \;=\; -\alpha\, g\big(g(x/\alpha)\big), \qquad \alpha = -\,\frac{1}{g(1)},
\]

i.e., $g$ is a fixed point of the period-doubling operator

\[
(\mathcal{T} g)(x) \;=\; -\alpha\, g\big(g(x/\alpha)\big).
\]

There is a unique such fixed point $g_*$ in the relevant class (Lanford 1982, computer-assisted; Campanino–Epstein 1981 by hand in a related class; Lyubich 1999 conceptually), with

\[
\alpha \;=\; -1/g_*(1) \;=\; 2.502907875095892822283\ldots
\]

The derivative $D\mathcal{T}_{g_*}$, acting on the appropriate space of even analytic perturbations modulo the directions generated by smooth coordinate changes, has exactly one eigenvalue outside the closed unit disk; it is simple and equals

\[
\delta \;=\; 4.669201609102990671853\ldots
\]

Universality: for any full unimodal family $f_\mu$ with quadratic critical point (e.g., $f_\mu(x) = \mu x(1-x)$), the period-doubling parameters $\mu_n$ satisfy

\[
\frac{\mu_n - \mu_{n-1}}{\mu_{n+1} - \mu_n} \;\longrightarrow\; \delta,
\]

and the attractor microstructure rescales by $-\alpha$. These are theorems for the quadratic universality class; the constants in this prompt always refer to that class.

### 1.2 The adopted question

**Adopted question.** *Determine the arithmetic nature of $\delta$ and $\alpha$: exhibit a proven closed form over a declared class of classical constants, or prove irrationality / transcendence, or prove non-membership in precisely defined closed-form classes.*

All three directions count. Note explicitly: a proven *nonexistence* of a closed form within a well-defined natural class is a legitimate resolution direction, not a consolation prize. Nothing along any of these lines is currently known for either constant - not even $\delta \notin \mathbb{Q}$.

### 1.3 Normalization pitfalls

Conventions differ across the literature: the sign of $\alpha$ (here $\alpha > 0$ with the minus sign explicit in the equation), the domain normalization $g(0) = 1$, and whether $\delta$ is defined via the operator eigenvalue or via superstable-parameter ratios. These definitions provably agree for the quadratic class, but a session must state which one it computes and, when comparing to a source, exhibit the identification. Mixing the quadratic-class constants with those of other critical orders is an error, not a convention.

## 2. Complete-resolution standard

Any one of the following, with complete proof, constitutes resolution:

1. **Closed form.** An exact expression for $\delta$ (or $\alpha$) over an explicitly declared, finitely generated class - algebraic numbers; closed forms over $\pi$, $e$, $\Gamma$ at rationals, zeta values; periods in the Kontsevich–Zagier sense - the class stated in the theorem, verified by a certified enclosure of both sides to margin exceeding the certification precision.
2. **Arithmetic-nature theorem.** A proof that $\delta$ (or $\alpha$) is irrational, or transcendental, or not a period - any unconditional non-membership in an infinite natural class.
3. **Class-nonexistence theorem.** A proof that $\delta$ (or $\alpha$) does not belong to a precisely defined *infinite* closed-form class (e.g., all algebraic numbers; the field generated by specified classical constants). Bounded-complexity exclusions (degree/height-bounded) are partial results (P3), not resolution, because the excluded class is finite.

**Not accepted as resolution**

- High-precision digits, certified or not, at any depth.
- PSLQ/LLL negative results presented as "evidence of transcendence" - they are exclusion certificates over finite classes only.
- Heuristic or statistical arguments about digit normality or apparent randomness.
- Non-rigorous numerics of the fixed point or the eigenvalue.
- Results about other universality classes (quartic critical points, circle maps, area-preserving period doubling) represented as statements about these constants.
- A closed-form candidate matching hundreds of digits without proof (report as conjecture, prominently labeled).

## 3. Graded partial-result targets

- **P1 - Independent certified pipeline: enclosures of $g_*$, $\alpha$, $\delta$.**
  - *Task:* rebuild Lanford's scheme on a modern stack. Target: $\ge 100$ certified digits of both constants, agreeing with published values.

  - *Value:* re-proves existence and hyperbolicity on our own auditable stack; every later target consumes it.

  - *Certificate:* the full-precision enclosure plus the checker run; wall-clock and memory curves recorded.

  - *Task:* before any mining, commit (timestamped) the constant bases - elementary closed forms; $\pi, e, \log 2, \zeta(3), \zeta(5)$, Catalan, $\Gamma(1/3), \Gamma(1/4)$ and products; cross-relations among $\delta$, $\alpha$, and auxiliary Feigenbaum quantities - plus polynomial degree/height budgets and detection thresholds with multiple-testing correction. Then run PSLQ/LLL at $\ge 2\times$ digit margin.
  - *Certificate:* machine-checkable exclusions: "any integer relation among $1, \delta, \delta^2, \ldots, \delta^d$ has norm $> H$" - rigorous PSLQ outputs given certified digits. Aggregate into the strongest honest statement short of section 2: *$\delta$ is not algebraic of degree $\le d$ and height $\le H$* for explicit large $d, H$.
  - *Value:* converts negative searches from folklore into citable theorems-with-certificates.
- **P4 - Structural theorems from the functional equation.**
  - *Task:* prove new unconditional properties of $g_*$ and $\delta$: certified enclosure of the analyticity domain and singularity structure of $g_*$ (Epstein-class a priori bounds sharpened by computation); sign/growth patterns of the $c_k$; identities linking $\delta$ to spectral determinants of the doubling operator. Goal: recast $\delta$ as a spectral/determinantal quantity concrete enough that transcendence machinery has a surface to grip - or prove no-go lemmas showing that specific classical frameworks (Mahler's method, E/G-function theory) *cannot* apply to the composition-type equation $g(x) = -\alpha g(g(x/\alpha))$ as posed.
  - *Certificate:* complete proofs; certified computations to the P1 standard; each no-go lemma a theorem, not an opinion.
  - *Value:* the only plausible route by which section 2 items 2–3 ever become reachable.
- **P5 - Irrationality-measure feasibility study, rigorously grounded.**
  - *Task:* determine whether any Apéry-style acceleration, continued-fraction structure, or functional-equation bootstrap yields rational approximations to $\delta$ fast enough to matter. Produce either (a) a proved irrationality criterion conditional on a checkable growth property of an explicitly constructed sequence, plus certified computation of that sequence deep enough to sharpen the conjecture, or (b) proofs that the candidate constructions fail.
  - *Certificate:* proofs plus certified sequence data.
  - *Value:* strongest realistic outcome short of section 2; even a well-posed conditional criterion would be the first foothold on irrationality.

Honest calibration: P1–P3 are achievable in-session; P4 partially; P5 is exploratory. The section 2 standards are decade-scale open mathematics, and the report must say so.

## 5. Attack plan

All under `[sym]`.

1. **Fixed-point enclosure (P1).** Represent $g$ by $N$ interval Taylor coefficients in $x^2$ on the disk $|x| < r$ (start $N \approx 40$, $r \approx 1.4$; scale $N$ with target digits). Compose rigorously in Arb (`arb_poly`/`acb_poly` with explicit tail buckets); Krawczyk step with the Jacobian of the truncated coefficient map; Cauchy-estimate tail control. Independent second implementation in C++/MPFR (kv-style intervals) for the checker. Sanity gate: $\alpha = -1/g(1)$ must land inside the published value's ball at every precision level.
2. **Eigenvalue enclosure (P1).** Assemble the truncated $D\mathcal{T}_{g_*}$ matrix in interval arithmetic; verified dominant eigenpair (interval power iteration or a verified dense eigensolver); rigorous bound pushing the remaining spectrum - including the truncation remainder, a compact operator with explicit norm bound - inside the unit disk. This simultaneously re-proves hyperbolicity on our own stack.
3. **Digit scaling (P2).** Newton in ball arithmetic doubles digits per step; cost is dominated by polynomial composition at $\mathcal{O}(10^4)$ digits and $N \sim 10^3$ coefficients - feasible on one workstation in days (FFT multiplication in FLINT). Record scaling curves for the report.
4. **Mining (P3).** PARI/GP `lindep` and mpmath `pslq`, run only per the pre-registered protocol; every run logged with basis, precision, budget, outcome; exclusion bounds extracted and archived as certificates; fplll as the independent LLL cross-check.
5. **Structure (P4).** SageMath/Mathematica manipulation of the functional equation for derivative identities and determinant representations; any claimed identity re-verified numerically at 100+ certified digits before proof effort is invested.

**First computations (session day one).**

1. Non-rigorous Newton on the truncated coefficient map ($N = 40$): recover $\alpha$ and $\delta$ to 30+ digits; confirm against OEIS values.
2. One complete certified run at low precision (20 digits): fixed-point enclosure, eigenvalue enclosure, checker pass - the full loop before scaling.
3. Convergence study: certified digits versus $(N, r, \text{precision})$ to calibrate the P2 schedule.
4. Dry-run PSLQ on a *known* constant (e.g., a planted algebraic number at matched precision) to validate the exclusion-certificate extraction before touching $\delta$.

**Workstation feasibility.**

- P1: hours. P2: days at $10^3$ digits; $10^4$ digits is the practical ceiling - memory and composition cost.
- P3: minutes per sweep; the discipline, not the compute, is the constraint.
- P4/P5: labor-bound, not compute-bound.

**Expected failure modes.**

- Ill-conditioning of high-degree coefficient maps; mitigate with scaled variables and staged precision.
- Spurious PSLQ hits at thin margins; the $2\times$ margin rule and mandatory re-test at higher precision exist for this.
- Universality-class confusion when harvesting literature values (quartic-critical constants; area-preserving doubling $\delta \approx 8.72$ is a different constant).
- Eigenvalue clustering in truncations making "the" $\delta$ eigenvalue ambiguous at low $N$; track eigenvector continuity across $N$.
- Tail-bound bookkeeping errors - the classic silent failure of functional-equation certificates; the independent checker must recompute tails from scratch.
