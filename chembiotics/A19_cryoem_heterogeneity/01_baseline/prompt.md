### Abstract

A cryo-EM dataset is hundreds of thousands of noisy 2D projections of a molecule frozen in many conformations at unknown orientations. Consensus reconstruction returns a single averaged map; *heterogeneous* reconstruction aims for the whole **distribution of conformations** - the object that actually carries the biology of a flexible machine. Methods like cryoDRGN (Zhong, Bhamre, Davis, Berger), 3D Variability Analysis (Punjani & Fleet, cryoSPARC), and RECOVAR (Gilles & Singer) learn a continuous latent conformational space, but on real data there is no ground truth to grade them against. This prompt is Pack A precisely because the loop can be closed with **synthetic ground truth**: simulate a *known* conformational distribution, render projections with realistic noise and CTF, and check *exact recovery* on-machine against the known latent, volumes, and populations. That known-answer comparison is the **on-machine verifier** - which is why this is an *algorithm-development* problem, not a biology problem. Biological insight from real data remains reality-gated (flag it). The central discipline is avoiding the inverse crime - grading a method on data generated by the very model it assumes - and handling the latent-space gauge freedom fairly. Anything short of the section-2 standard - a pretty latent embedding with no metric against known ground truth, or a match under a matched forward model only - is a partial result, never a validated recovery.

## 1. Exact problem statement

**Forward model.** Each image is

\[
y_i \;=\; \mathrm{CTF}_i \ast \big[\,\Pi(R_i)\, V(z_i)\,\big] \;+\; \eta_i,
\]

where:

- $V(z)$ is the 3D scattering volume as a function of a conformational coordinate $z$;
- $z_i \sim p^\star(z)$ is drawn from the ground-truth distribution;
- $R_i$ is the (a-priori unknown) pose (rotation and in-plane shift);
- $\Pi$ is tomographic projection;
- $\mathrm{CTF}_i$ is the per-image contrast transfer function (defocus, and optionally astigmatism);
- $\eta_i$ is noise at a specified signal-to-noise ratio.

Whether poses and CTF are given (pose-supervised) or estimated (ab initio) is stated per experiment.

**Synthetic ground truth.** A committed conformational ensemble with *known* $p^\star(z)$:

- a 1D reaction coordinate interpolating two atomic models, a set of normal-mode displacements, an MD-derived trajectory, or discrete states with known populations;
- from it, a dataset of $N$ images is rendered with recorded $\{z_i, R_i, \mathrm{CTF}_i, \eta_i\}$;
- the ensemble and dataset are frozen and hashed before any method is run.

**Recovery task.** From the images (and, per the setting, poses/CTF), recover:

- the per-particle latent $\hat z_i$;
- the volume-generating map / the distribution $\hat p(z)$;
- reconstructed volumes $V(\hat z)$.

**Metrics (numeric, because ground truth is known).**

- **Latent recovery:** correlation between $\hat z$ and $z$ up to the intrinsic gauge freedom - evaluated by a *committed* alignment (canonical correlation, or an optimal monotone/affine reparameterization stated in advance), never an arbitrary post-hoc nonlinear warp.
- **Volume accuracy:** Fourier Shell Correlation (FSC) between $V(\hat z)$ and the ground-truth $V(z)$ at matched $z$, reported as resolution at FSC $= 0.5$ and $0.143$.
- **Distribution recovery:** a distance (Wasserstein or KL) between $\hat p(z)$ and $p^\star(z)$ after the committed alignment.

**Accuracy threshold.** Numeric and reported *as a function of SNR and particle count*: e.g. latent canonical correlation $\ge \rho_{\min}$; ensemble-averaged FSC resolution $\le r_{\min}$ Å; distribution distance $\le \tau$. A single favourable operating point is not a target; the recovery curve is.

## 2. Resolution standard

Full resolution is a heterogeneous-reconstruction method with a *certified recovery guarantee on synthetic ground truth*: across a committed set of phantoms and a swept SNR/particle-count range, it recovers the latent, the per-conformation volumes, and the population distribution to committed thresholds under the committed gauge-alignment, with all metrics recomputed by a standalone evaluator separate from the reconstruction code - and, critically, the guarantee *survives model mismatch* (a generator strictly more realistic than the method assumes). Because the biology of any real complex is reality-gated, resolution here is an algorithmic guarantee on synthetic data, stated as such.

**Not accepted as resolution:**

- A latent embedding shown as a pretty 2D scatter with no quantitative metric against known ground truth.
- Recovery demonstrated only under the **inverse crime** - data generated by the exact forward model the reconstruction assumes - with no mismatched-generator test.
- Latent "recovery" achieved only after an arbitrary, uncommitted nonlinear reparameterization that could align noise (gauge cheating).
- A single-SNR, single-phantom result generalized to a claim of robust recovery.
- Consensus-map quality (a single averaged volume) presented as recovery of the *distribution*.
- Synthetic-data success presented as a biological finding on real data (see integrity clause).

**Benchmark-integrity clause.** The synthetic ground truth is generated by *a* forward model, and grading a method on data from *its own* model is the inverse crime - a confident-but-wrong success. The mandatory guards:

- a **held-out mismatched generator** strictly more realistic than any reconstruction model (structural/solvent noise, realistic CTF with astigmatism, non-Gaussian or per-particle-varying noise, junk/outlier particles, and pose-estimation error), on which recovery is re-evaluated and degradation reported;
- a **committed gauge alignment** fixed before evaluation, so latent-correlation numbers cannot be inflated by post-hoc warping;
- multiple **phantoms spanning motion types** (continuous 1D, multi-dimensional, discrete-state), so a method tuned to one motion is exposed.

Real-data biological claims are *not* validated by any of this and must be flagged as reality-gated. A recovery that holds only under a matched forward model, or only after gauge cheating, is confident-but-wrong and must be flagged as such.

## 3. Graded partial-result targets

- **P1 (verified pipeline reproduces a cryoDRGN-style recovery).** On a committed synthetic phantom with known ground truth (a 2-state or 1D-motion ensemble, or a published simulated set), run a cryoDRGN-style method and recover the latent and per-conformation volumes; report latent correlation, FSC resolution, and distribution distance. *Certificate:* the phantom generator + the reconstruction + the standalone metric computation, reproducible from a manifest.
- **P2 (certified recovery vs SNR).** With the ground truth frozen, report recovery metrics as a function of SNR and particle count - the recovery curve, with error bars. *Certificate:* the frozen phantom + the metric-vs-SNR table + an independent recomputation of every metric.
- **P3 (robustness under model mismatch).** Re-evaluate P1/P2 on the mismatched, more-realistic generator (structural noise, junk particles, pose error, astigmatic CTF); report the degradation relative to the matched case. *Certificate:* the mismatched-generator phantom + the paired metrics.
- **P4 (algorithmic improvement on held-out synthetic data).** A method change (architecture, regularization, or estimator) that beats a committed baseline on a *frozen held-out* phantom by a certified margin under the committed metric and gauge. *Certificate:* the frozen split + the baseline and improved metrics + the significance of the margin.
- **P5 (uncertainty quantification on recovered ensembles).** Calibrated uncertainty on $\hat p(z)$ or per-voxel, validated against known ground truth by coverage/calibration on synthetic data (does a claimed 90% interval contain the truth 90% of the time?). *Certificate:* calibration curves versus the known ensemble.

Full resolution (a method with a mismatch-surviving, calibrated recovery guarantee across phantoms and SNR) is unlikely in one session; P1–P2 are realistic, P3–P4 are the target, P5 is the stretch.

- Gilles & Singer ~2023–2024 - RECOVAR, regularized covariance / principled PCA estimation of the conformational subspace (verify venue/year).
- Chen & Ludtke ~2021 - e2gmm (EMAN2), Gaussian-mixture heterogeneity; Dashti, Frank ~2014–2020 - ManifoldEM, manifold embedding of conformations.

## 5. Attack plan

**[data] Ground-truth generation.** Build phantoms from atomic models (PDB) rendered to density (pdb2mrc / molmap-style), with motions from normal modes (ProDy) or a short MD trajectory, or discrete states with set populations; render projections with poses, CTF (defocus $\pm$ astigmatism), and noise using cryoDRGN's simulator or CryoBench. Commit and hash the ground-truth ensemble and dataset before any reconstruction. Build a *second, more realistic* generator for the P3 mismatch test.

**[struct] Reconstruction and metrics.** cryoDRGN (PyTorch) as the primary continuous-latent method; RELION consensus as the null baseline; 3DVA/RECOVAR as linear-subspace comparators. The metric evaluator is standalone: it takes recovered latents/volumes and the frozen ground truth and computes latent canonical correlation under the committed alignment, FSC-at-matched-$z$, and the distribution distance - with the gauge alignment fixed before it ever sees a result.

**One-workstation scope.** cryoDRGN trains on $\sim 10^5$ particles on a single prosumer GPU in hours; phantom generation and metric evaluation are CPU-cheap; the full SNR sweep is a multi-day single-workstation campaign. **Failure modes:** the inverse crime (guarded by the mismatched generator); latent gauge ambiguity (guarded by the committed alignment); pose-estimation error dominating in the ab-initio setting; mode collapse / smearing that averages away the distribution; insufficient particles at low SNR; GPU memory limits on box size; and overfitting to a single phantom (guarded by the multi-phantom, multi-motion set).

## 6. Verification and auditability requirements

1. **Known-answer, gauge-fair numerics.** Every recovery number is computed against the frozen synthetic ground truth by a standalone evaluator under a gauge alignment committed before evaluation; metrics are reported as SNR/particle-count curves with error bars, not single points; floating-point qualitative embeddings never count as certified recovery.
2. **Independent verification.** The metric evaluator is written separately from the reconstruction code; headline results are recomputed from saved latents/volumes; the mismatched-generator re-evaluation is an independent check on inverse-crime inflation.
3. **Reproducibility.** The ground-truth ensemble, the forward-model parameters (SNR, CTF, noise model), both generators, method versions, seeds, and the committed gauge alignment and train/held-out split are recorded; a SHA-256 manifest covers every phantom, dataset, checkpoint, and metric file, committed before evaluation.
4. **Preservation.** Phantom generators (matched and mismatched), reconstruction configs and checkpoints, the evaluator, and failed/rejected runs are part of the record; anything discarded is listed as discarded.
5. **Honest reporting.** The report states up front that results are algorithmic guarantees on synthetic data, reports the mismatched-generator (not just matched) metrics and the committed-gauge (not post-hoc-warped) correlations, distinguishes distribution recovery from consensus-map quality, and never presents a synthetic recovery as a validated biological finding on real data.
