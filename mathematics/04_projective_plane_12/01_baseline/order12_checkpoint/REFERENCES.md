# References checked

1. B. Alexeev and D. G. Mixon, *Forbidden Sidon subsets of perfect difference sets, featuring a human-assisted proof*, arXiv:2510.19804 (2025), Section 4. It records that order 12 remains the smallest unresolved projective-plane order.
2. K. Akiyama, C. Suetake, and M. Tanaka, *Projective planes of order 12 do not have a collineation group of order 4*, Journal of Combinatorial Designs 31 (2023), 87-123. DOI: 10.1002/jcd.21869.
3. H. Kharaghani and S. Suda, *Hadamard Matrices related to Projective Planes*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 30(2) (2023), P2.49. DOI: 10.37236/11990.
4. M. De Boeck and G. Van de Voorde, *Embedded antipodal planes and the minimum weight of the dual code of points and lines in projective planes of order p^2*, Designs, Codes and Cryptography 91 (2023), 895-920. DOI: 10.1007/s10623-022-01131-2. This is background on small dual-code words; the order-12 derivations in this package are direct and do not assume prime-power order.
5. Z. Janko and T. van Trung, *On projective planes of order 12 which have a subplane of order 3, I*, Journal of Combinatorial Theory A 29 (1980), 254-256. DOI: 10.1016/0097-3165(80)90017-5.
