## Governing separation

**Open, reachable, and valuable are independent tests.** A target does not become reachable merely because a witness is cheap to verify. A seconds-long checker is an admission condition: it prevents unverifiable search. It is not a tractability forecast.

Stage 0 therefore does not search for witnesses. It freezes the claim, freshness evidence, artifact grammar, checker, baseline, search edge, budget, kill rules, and publication path. Compute is admitted only when all nine gates are green.

## Target-selection bias

Prefer recent, explicit, finite claims from low-traffic sources where:

- one finite object, coefficient sequence, parameter row, or lemma settles the obligation;
- the source has a replayable computational frontier or a sharply delimited theorem boundary;
- a new representation removes work rather than merely adding compute;
- an author, maintainer, or specialist venue can validate and absorb a result.

Reject famous named conjectures, machine-conjecture feeds, mature record races, broad asymptotic questions, workshop lists dominated by proof-only problems, and claims whose status or quantifiers cannot be frozen.

## What counts as a search edge

A search edge must be operational and comparative. Valid forms include a symmetry quotient, canonical representation, exact certificate format, decomposition, problem-specific dynamic program, new data/corpus, or solver formulation that the baseline did not use. “Use more compute,” “try an LLM,” and “enumerate further” are not edges.

Before attack, benchmark the edge against the reproduced baseline. A plausible but unbenchmarked idea remains `needs-edge`.

## Priority without scalar collapse

Keep the eight-dimensional vector:

1. open-status confidence;
2. reachability;
3. method advantage;
4. witness/lemma plausibility;
5. scientific value;
6. verification quality;
7. competition penalty, where 5 means low competition;
8. end-to-end cost, where 5 means cheap.

Do not sum the vector. Ranking is a documented portfolio judgment, not arithmetic camouflage.

## Deep-attack discipline

- Refresh status immediately before compute.
- Reproduce baseline first; otherwise reclassify as infrastructure.
- Generate only artifacts accepted by the independent checker grammar.
- Freeze on implementation disagreement.
- Review method after 20% of budget without structural information.
- Stop on repeated plateau without a changed representation.
- Stop when measured growth exceeds the fixed forecast by the card’s factor.
- Blind-verify any claimed result, then perform statement-match, timestamped-open-status, novelty, and expert-review checks.

Persistence means changing hypotheses and representations, not repeatedly sampling the same search distribution.
