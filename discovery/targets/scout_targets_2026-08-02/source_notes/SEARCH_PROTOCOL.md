# Source-search protocol

The scout used exact-title and identifier searches first, then conjecture-specific phrases, author names, citation/follow-up terms, and source-linked repositories or supplements. Recent source versions were preferred over snippets. PDF pages containing conjectures/tables were visually checked where HTML did not expose the relevant statement.

No source PDF is mirrored here. Primary locators are preserved inside every card to avoid distributing stale copies and to keep version identity explicit.
