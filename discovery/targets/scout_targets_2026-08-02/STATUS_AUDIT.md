## Protocol

Access date for every card is 2026-08-02. Searches used the exact title, arXiv identifier or DOI, author names, conjecture/table locator, distinctive formula/keywords, and follow-up terms such as proof, counterexample, resolution, and citation. The primary source was checked to confirm that it still presented the item as conjectural or unverified. Search-negative evidence is not logically exhaustive; older or ambiguous cases are therefore red.

## Explicit nonclaims

“No resolution found” means no resolution surfaced in the documented web audit. It does not cover private correspondence, unindexed manuscripts, or future publications. Every Stage 1 session must repeat the audit on the day compute begins. Cards marked `needs-status` require stronger evidence, preferably author/maintainer confirmation or a complete, recent citation trail.
